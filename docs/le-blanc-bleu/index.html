About

Only four bungalows

Le Blanc Bleu is a private guest house of just four bungalows, built in the eighteenth century in the old-cellar style and kept in their vintage character. Each one overlooks the Mediterranean and opens onto direct access to the beach. Rooms are spacious and well equipped, rented by the day, the week, or the month.
